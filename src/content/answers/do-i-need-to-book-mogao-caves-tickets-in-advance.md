The Mogao Caves are conserved rather than merely displayed, and the ticketing reflects that. Visitor numbers are capped because breath and body heat damage 1,600-year-old pigment.

## The booking rules

Tickets are released **up to 30 days ahead** through the **Dunhuang Academy's official booking site and its WeChat mini-programme**. Booking is **real-name** — you enter passport or ID details and must carry that same document to the entrance.

The Academy has been explicit that it has **authorised no third-party sellers**. Agency listings offering Mogao tickets are reselling access to the same public system, and a booking made under someone else's name is not transferable to yours.

There is no walk-up ticket office to fall back on. In high season, treat availability as the constraint that shapes your Dunhuang dates rather than something to sort out on arrival.

## What a visit actually consists of

You do not choose your caves. A standard visit is:

1. Two films at the **Digital Exhibition Centre**, including a dome projection of caves you will not enter.
2. A shuttle to the cliff face.
3. A **guided visit to about eight caves**, selected by the Academy on the day and rotated to spread wear.

Photography inside is prohibited, and this is enforced. The rotation means two visitors on consecutive days see different caves, so there is no point trying to engineer a particular one.

## Why the numbers are worth knowing

There are **735 caves** in the cliff, of which **492 preserve wall painting and sculpture** — roughly 45,000 square metres of murals, the greatest surviving body of Buddhist wall painting anywhere. English-language tours are fewer than Chinese ones, which tightens availability further on the dates you can actually use.

## Book the station before you book the hotel

The other Dunhuang trap is transport. The high-speed stop that carries the region's name is **Liuyuan South**, and it is about **130 km from the town** — a real transfer, not a taxi hop. Dunhuang has its own station much closer in, on a conventional line. Check which station your ticket names before you book anything else, because the two are not interchangeable and the mistake costs a half-day.

## If it is sold out

- **Move the date rather than the plan.** Dunhuang is worth restructuring a Silk Road itinerary around; the caves are not a substitutable sight.
- **Check for released cancellations** as the date approaches.
- **The Digital Exhibition Centre films** are genuinely good and are part of the standard ticket, so a visit is not only the eight caves.
