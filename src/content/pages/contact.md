## Corrections

Found a price that has moved, a sight that has closed, a train that no longer runs or a rule that has changed? Tell us. Include the page and, where you can, the source — an official website, a ticket, a photograph of a sign.

Corrections are our fastest route to accuracy and we credit readers who want credit.

## Questions about a trip

We read everything and answer what we can, but we are a small operation and cannot plan individual itineraries or provide visa advice for a specific case. For entry questions, the Chinese embassy or consulate in your country is the only authoritative source — and the answer genuinely does depend on your nationality and your exact route.

Most trip questions are already answered somewhere on the site. Start with:

- [The first-timer's guide](/plan/first-time-china-travel-guide/)
- [Visas and visa-free entry](/guides/china-visa-guide/)
- [Money and payments](/guides/money-and-payments-in-china/)
- [Search the whole site](/search/)

## Partnerships and press

We accept affiliate partnerships in the categories described in our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) and display advertising through standard networks.

We do not accept paid placement, sponsored posts presented as editorial, or link insertions into existing articles. Please do not ask; the answer will not change.

## How to reach us

Email is the only channel we monitor. See our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/) for how we handle corrections and updates.

*Set your working address in `src/content/site.mjs` before launch — it is rendered from `SITE.contactEmail` and appears in the site's structured data.*
