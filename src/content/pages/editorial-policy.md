## Independence

**No paid placement, ever.** No destination, hotel, restaurant, tour operator or brand can pay to appear in a guide, to be ranked higher, or to be described more favourably. Commercial relationships exist only through the affiliate and advertising arrangements described in our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/), and they do not influence what we recommend.

**We recommend against our own commercial interest** where the facts require it. Several pages on this site tell readers to choose the cheaper non-commissionable option. That is deliberate and it is the point.

**Sponsored content, if it ever appears, is labelled as such** at the top of the page, in the same size type as the body text.

## Accuracy

- **Primary sources.** Visa and entry information comes from Chinese embassy and National Immigration Administration announcements. Prices and hours come from official booking systems. Where we cite a figure with a policy attached, we say when it was checked.
- **No invented specifics.** We do not publish prices, times or rules we have not verified. Where something varies or is uncertain, we say so rather than picking a plausible number.
- **Dates on everything.** Every page carries a last-updated date.
- **Expiry flags.** Chinese visa-free schemes carry trial periods and end dates. We flag them and tell readers to confirm before booking.

## Corrections

If we publish something wrong, we fix it and — where the error was material — note the correction on the page. Reader corrections are welcome and are the fastest way we find problems. Send them via the [contact page](/contact/).

## Review and updates

- **Entry, payment and connectivity guides** are reviewed at least quarterly, and immediately when a policy changes.
- **Destination and itinerary guides** are reviewed annually and after any significant change to a major sight's access or ticketing.
- **Prices** are refreshed annually; readers should still treat them as indicative.

## Use of AI

Drafting tools may be used in production. Every published page is reviewed by a human before publication, and factual claims — prices, times, rules, distances — are checked against sources rather than accepted from a model. We do not publish machine-generated text unreviewed, and we do not generate fake reviews, fake photographs or fabricated first-person experiences.

## Sensitive coverage

China includes places and topics where travel writing has consequences for the people who live there. Our approach:

- We describe entry requirements, security procedures and access restrictions factually, because travellers need to plan around them.
- We do not encourage visitors to photograph security infrastructure, press local people on political subjects, or behave in ways that create risk for someone who cannot leave.
- We link to official government travel advice rather than substituting our judgement for it.

## What we are not

We are not a travel agency, we do not sell tours, and we do not operate on the ground in China. When a guide recommends using a licensed agency — for a Tibet permit, for instance — we have no stake in which one you choose.
